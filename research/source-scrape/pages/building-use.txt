Building Use

Our beautiful church has many spaces suitable for a variety functions: weddings, receptions, group meals, family gatherings, and meetings. We encourage you to come see the space!
Request use of our building by contacting the church office (info@firstcongregationalstalbans.org).
-Sanctuary (seats 350; grand piano)
-Library (1-5 people)
-Fellowship Hall/Chapel (fits approx. 50; baby grand piano)
-Kitchen (two 80-cup coffee percolators, 4 ovens, 2 ranges, microwave, 3 sinks, and 2 refrigerators)
-Parlor (up to 20 people)
This slideshow requires JavaScript.

Planning a church wedding? We are a welcoming and affirming congregation. Not only do we have a beautiful space inside which can accommodate a reception, our church faces Taylor Park and its pavilion, fountain, and gardens. Booking fees vary, as it depends on the demand of lighting and heat. You are required to provide your own set up and clean up, and maintain building security for the members of your group. The building and grounds are smoke free and alcohol free. You are welcome to stop by during office hours and view our building and grounds with either our Minister or the Church Secretary.
THANK YOU for considering our building and grounds for your event! We look forward to seeing you and helping to plan!
