About Us

No matter who you are, or where you are on life’s journey, you are welcome here.
First Congregational Church is an Open & Affirming congregation.

Worship Services Sunday 10:00am
We are an intimate and friendly group who worship weekly in our historic, downtown church building. Each Sunday the choir and the organ provide music for meditation and hymn-singing. Guests are always welcome and are encouraged to join us for fellowship time (coffee and treats!) following the hour-long service. Our church service is broadcast over Channel 1079 formerly known as Channel 15, Public Access TV on Sundays at 4:00 PM.
You are invited to phone or email our church office for additional information.
Our church is located in the heart of downtown St. Albans, adjacent to Taylor Park, the town green, and our clock tower has chimed for the community since 1893. Entrances are from Church Street or the parking lot in the rear. Nursery can be arranged for any young person(s), but they are also welcome to remain in the worship service.
In the Sanctuary on Sunday morning Ongoing events: -Church Street Sounds of the Season music series in Fall -Prayer Chain -Bible Study -Choir Rehearsals -Sunday Potluck Luncheons -Ladies’ Breakfasts and Men’s Breakfasts -Red Cross Blood Drives -Weekly A.A. meetings Call the church office for more information: 802-524-4555.
Office Hours: Tuesday -Friday: 9am – 2pm
