Lenten Meditation Walk
March 30, 2021 Sunday Bulletin firstcongregationalstalbansvt

If you are looking for a moment to reflect during Holy Week, please feel free to download FCC’s Lenten Meditation Walk.
The meditation walk will guide you through Taylor Park here in St. Albans. You can do the walk at any time, but if you do the walk on Good Friday between 4-5pm you can finish your meditation in silent prayer while listening to our organist, Stephan Conrady, play meditations.
