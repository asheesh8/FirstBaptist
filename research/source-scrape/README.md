# Public source archive

Snapshot of the public content at [firstcongregationalstalbans.org](https://firstcongregationalstalbans.org/), captured on August 18, 2026 for content migration and brand research.

## Captured material

- 220 sitemap-listed pages downloaded as original HTML and extracted plain text
- 73 church-hosted images downloaded at their original available URLs
- 214 linked documents downloaded, primarily Sunday bulletins plus four historical reference PDFs
- 42 images classified as large enough for strong web use, 4 as medium, 7 as limited, and 20 as small/thumbnail assets
- 198,305,389 bytes of downloaded source assets; the complete archive is approximately 211 MB
- Public Mailchimp RSS snapshot for the future `What's Happening` feed

The asset manifest contains 207 additional image URLs that were deliberately not downloaded. Nearly all are YouTube-generated video thumbnails hosted outside the church domain; one is the external UCC affiliation mark and one is a WordPress tracking pixel. Their source URLs remain recorded in `assets.json` and `assets.csv`.

## Important files

- `summary.json` - archive totals and errors
- `pages.json` / `pages.csv` - URL, modification date, title, local copies, and linked-media counts
- `assets.json` / `assets.csv` - every discovered image/document URL and download result
- `sitemap-images.json` - image titles and captions supplied by the WordPress sitemap
- `image-inventory.json` / `image-inventory.csv` - dimensions and broad web-suitability assessment
- `pages/` - source HTML and extracted text
- `assets/images/` - original church-hosted images
- `assets/documents/` - linked bulletins and historical PDFs
- `history-documents/` - extracted and rendered versions of the history, ministers, clock, and windows PDFs
- `contact-sheets/` - five visual overview sheets of archived images
- `mailchimp-feed.xml` - ten most recent public Mailchimp items at capture time

## Migration notes

- This is a preservation and research archive, not a ready-to-publish media library. Confirm current staff, event, program, and office-hour information before launch.
- Several source images are legacy or orphaned WordPress media and do not represent current staff or current programs.
- Public availability is not a substitute for the church confirming its publication rights and consent practices, especially for recognizable children.
- Preserve attribution when the source identifies a photographer or historian.
