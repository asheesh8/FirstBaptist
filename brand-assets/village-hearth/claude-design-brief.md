# First Congregational Church of St. Albans — design brief

## Brand

**Organization:** First Congregational Church of St. Albans, Vermont  
**Design-system name:** Village Hearth  
**Emotional promise:** There's room for you here.  
**Supporting line:** A town landmark. An open door.

First Congregational should feel like a beloved Vermont community institution with modern product-level clarity: historic without being dusty, joyful without becoming childish, and spiritually open without becoming vague. Its voice welcomes seekers, believers, and doubters with plain language, practical information, a little warmth, and no pressure.

## Logo

The `Welcome Chime` mark combines the church's Romanesque rounded architecture, four-faced town clock, and a wide open doorway. Use the formal face-free mark for identity; reserve any future bell character or expressive animation for helpful interface moments, never the official seal.

## Type

- Display and identity: **Fraunces**.
- Interface and long-form copy: **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next**.
- Use generous line spacing, sentence case, and short headings. Avoid all-caps except small metadata labels.

## Tone words

Joyful, neighborly, historic, clear, inclusive, musical, homey, contemplative.

## Do

- Lead with Sunday time, location, parking/access information, and one reassuring next step.
- Use authentic church and community photography with warm natural color.
- Let the mark, architecture, clock, brick, stained glass, music, and Vermont seasons create the distinctiveness.

## Don't

- Do not copy Duolingo's owl, green, type, button depth, streaks, or character proportions.
- Do not use generic megachurch black-and-gold styling, glowing crosses, fake congregants, or AI-generated children.
- Do not gamify attendance or giving; delight belongs in welcome and confirmation moments, not pressure.
