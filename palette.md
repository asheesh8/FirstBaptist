# Bell & Hearth palette

Approved August 18, 2026 for the `Village Hearth` brand direction.

| Color | Hex | Intended role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meetinghouse Paper | `#F7F1E4` | Main warm canvas, quiet cards, and photography margins |
| Spruce Ink | `#183B35` | Primary text, navigation, footer, and dark surfaces |
| Hearth Brick | `#B4472D` | Primary action, logo emphasis, and historic-building signature |
| Taylor Green | `#5C8664` | Programs, community, landscape, and soft supporting surfaces |
| Chime Gold | `#E5B642` | Dates, celebration, small highlights, and motion moments |
| Tiffany Sky | `#6AAFC0` | Reflection, music, calm accents, and links on dark surfaces |

## Accessibility assignments

- Default text: Spruce Ink on Meetinghouse Paper - approximately `10.87:1` contrast.
- Primary button: white on Hearth Brick - approximately `5.41:1` contrast.
- Secondary dark treatment: Chime Gold on Spruce Ink - approximately `6.47:1` contrast.
- Tiffany Sky on Spruce Ink - approximately `4.96:1` contrast.
- Taylor Green is decorative/supporting and should not carry small text without a separately verified contrast pairing.

## Usage rules

- Use Meetinghouse Paper and Spruce Ink for most of the interface.
- Reserve Hearth Brick for the main action in a viewport; do not make every link red.
- Use Chime Gold for brief celebratory moments, not long text or large background fields.
- Use Tiffany Sky primarily for music, reflection, and calm informational states.
- An inclusive rainbow may appear as a restrained Open and Affirming signal. It is not a replacement for the functional palette and should not turn every interface element into a competing accent.
- Do not introduce Duolingo green, generic black-and-gold megachurch styling, neon gradients, or unrelated seasonal colors without extending this palette intentionally.
